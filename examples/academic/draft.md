# Antenatal Care Utilisation in Rural Districts

## Introduction

Antenatal care reduces maternal and newborn risk, yet attendance in rural districts remains low. This chapter reports a mixed-methods study of the barriers women describe and the patterns visible in clinic records.

## Research Questions

What are the factors influencing utilisation of antenatal care in rural districts?

How do travel distance and cost affect attendance at antenatal clinics?

## Methodology

We reviewed 1,240 clinic records and interviewed 36 women — a purposive sample drawn from three districts. Interviews were transcribed and coded by two analysts, who resolved disagreements by discussion.

## Findings

Attendance was highest where transport support existed. Cost was named more often than distance. It is important to note that these findings are associative rather than causal.
